About Us

Hark-Prints is more than just a custom print shop; it is a family-driven venture built on a foundation of personal connection. Our name, HARK, is an acronym representing the first initials of our family members—the very people who inspire us to create every day. We believe that whether you are wearing a custom hoodie or gifting a personalized planner, your gear should reflect the things you value most. From our family to yours, we are dedicated to bringing your vision to life with quality and care.

Ready to make a statement? At Hark-Prints, our mission is simple: Get pressed. Get noticed. We specialize in custom apparel and promotional goods, including t-shirts, hoodies, hats, and party favors. Using DTF and HTV prints, we help bring your vision to life.
